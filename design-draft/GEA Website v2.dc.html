02 — Our story

GEA Haulage and Logistics was established with a clear purpose: to provide dependable transportation and logistics services that businesses can trust.

Recognizing the importance of timely and secure goods transportation, we are committed to helping businesses overcome logistics challenges through professional service, careful planning, and a customer-focused approach.

Our journey is driven by a passion for service excellence and a determination to contribute to the growth of businesses through reliable haulage solutions. As we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to move goods responsibly, serve our customers faithfully, and build a reputation founded on trust and excellence.

Our mission

To provide safe, reliable, efficient, and cost-effective haulage and logistics services that meet our customers' needs, support business growth, and deliver exceptional value through professionalism and integrity.

Our vision

To become one of the most trusted and respected haulage and logistics companies in Nigeria, recognized for service excellence, operational efficiency, safety, and customer satisfaction.
