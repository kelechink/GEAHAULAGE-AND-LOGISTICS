What we haul

One company for the whole job, from the port or factory gate to the final drop.

  • General haulage

    Palletised, bagged and boxed goods from factories, markets and depots, on trucks sized to your load.

  • Container haulage

    20ft and 40ft containers moved from Apapa and Tin Can ports to your warehouse, and empties returned.

  • Petroleum products

    Tanker haulage of fuel and other petroleum products between depots and stations.

  • Bulk and dry cargo

    Cement, grain, fertiliser and other bulk loads moved in full truckloads.

  • Heavy equipment

    Lowbed and flatbed trucks for machinery, generators and construction plant.

  • Warehousing and storage

    Hold goods with us between arrival and delivery, so you aren't paying port storage charges.

  • Freight forwarding and clearing

    We handle the paperwork to get cargo out of the port, then deliver it on our own trucks.

  • Interstate deliveries

    Scheduled and one-off runs between Lagos and any state in Nigeria.

How booking works

  1. Tell us the load

    Send the cargo, weight, pick-up and delivery towns through the quote form, WhatsApp or a call.

  2. Get a truck and a price

    We pick the right truck from our 5 to 30 tonne fleet and confirm the price and loading date.

  3. Load and follow it

    Your goods are loaded at the agreed time, and you can call or message us for updates on the road.

  4. Delivery and sign-off

    Your receiver checks the goods and signs the waybill. We send you confirmation once it's done.

About GEA

GEA Haulage and Logistics was set up in 2020 with one purpose: to give businesses transport they can depend on. Getting goods where they need to be, safely and on time, is part of every business that works, so that's what we focus on.

We run our own fleet of 35 trucks, from 5 to 30 tonnes, from our office and yard in Alagbado, Lagos. We treat every delivery as a commitment and every client as a long-term partner.

Our mission

To provide safe, reliable, efficient and cost-effective haulage and logistics services that meet our customers' needs, support business growth, and deliver real value through professionalism and integrity.

Our vision

To become one of the most trusted and respected haulage and logistics companies in Nigeria, known for service excellence, operational efficiency, safety and customer satisfaction.
