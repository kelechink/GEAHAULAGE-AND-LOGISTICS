# Management guide

You can see everything: the dashboard, CRM, shipments, fleet, finance, reports and the website assistant. You approve discounts over 10% and void invoices.

## Every morning

1. At 7am you get the **morning briefing** email: active and quiet trips, delays with reasons, pickups and deliveries today, overdue invoices with totals, documents expiring in 14 days, new and hot leads, missed follow-ups. The same briefing is at the top of the dashboard.
2. Open the **Dashboard**. The coloured tiles show income, expenses and net cash this month, money owed to GEA, quote requests this week, deliveries with the on-time share, and active trips. **Needs attention** lists what to act on, each with a link.
3. Click the **bell** (top right) for alerts: delayed trips, trips with no update for an hour, overdue invoices, leads not called within a working day, driver breakdowns, and website chats waiting for a person.

## Ask the system

**Assistant → Ask the system** answers plain questions with a report, for example:

- "Which trucks made the most money in October?"
- "List customers owing more than ₦500k"
- "Which documents expire in the next 30 days?"

The AI only reads your question to choose the report. The figures are worked out by the system and never sent to the AI.

## Approvals

- **Discounts over 10%:** Finance can draft the invoice, but only Management can issue it. Open the invoice and click **Issue invoice**.
- **Voiding an invoice:** open it and click **Void**, with a reason. A paid invoice needs a credit note first.

## Reports

- **Finance → Finance reports:** revenue by month, customer and route; expenses by category; profit per truck and per trip; receivables ageing; VAT and WHT. Download as CSV, PDF or Word.
- **CRM → CRM reports:** leads by source, conversion by stage, response time, won value by month.

## Website assistant

**Assistant → Conversations** shows every website chat. Chats marked **Needs a person** are waiting for staff (Sales are alerted). **Assistant → Knowledge** is everything the assistant may say. Keep it accurate: it never invents prices or policies, but it repeats what is written there.
