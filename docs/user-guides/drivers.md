# GEA Driver app

For drivers with an Android phone. If you don't have one, Operations will call you for updates instead.

## Getting started

1. Operations installs the **GEA Driver** app on your phone and gives you a **PIN**.
2. Open the app, enter your phone number and PIN, tap **Sign in**.
3. Allow **location "all the time"**, the **camera** and **notifications** when asked. Location is only used while a trip is active, so GEA and the customer can follow the truck.

## Your trips

**My trips** lists the trips assigned to you. Tap one to see the customer, cargo, pickup date, waybill and ETA. **Call Operations** rings the office. **Open directions** opens the map.

## The big button

Tap the big button at each step. It always shows the next step:

**Start loading → Loading done → Start trip → I have arrived → Confirm delivery**

While the trip is on the road, a notification says **Trip tracking is on**. The app sends your location every minute while moving and every 5 minutes while parked.

## Confirm delivery

1. Type the **receiver's name**.
2. Tap **Take photo** of the goods or the signed waybill, and/or ask the receiver to **sign on the screen**.
3. Tap **Confirm delivery**.

## Report a problem

Tap **Report a problem**, choose **Breakdown, Accident, Delay or Checkpoint**, say what happened (needed for a delay), add a photo if you can, and tap **Send to Operations**. Operations is alerted straight away and will call you.

## No network?

Keep working. Everything you do is saved on the phone and sent automatically when you have signal. The trip shows **not sent yet** until then. If the office refuses an update, the app tells you: call Operations.

## Problems signing in

Wrong PIN five times locks sign-in for a minute. If you forget your PIN or change phones, ask Operations for a new one.
