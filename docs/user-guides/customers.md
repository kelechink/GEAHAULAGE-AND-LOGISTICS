# GEA customer portal

For GEA's customers. Sign in at `https://geahaulageandlogistics.com/portal/login`.

## Getting your account

GEA invites you by email ("Your GEA customer portal account", from support@geahaulageandlogistics.com). Click **Choose your password**, pick a password of at least 10 characters with letters and numbers, then sign in with your email. The link works for 60 minutes; if it expired, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

## What you can see

- **Active shipments:** where each one is, its stage and the expected delivery window. **Track** opens the live map.
- **Invoices:** every issued invoice with its total, balance and status. **PDF** opens it to print or save. Use the invoice number as your payment reference.
- **Quotes:** quotes GEA has sent you, as PDFs.
- **Past shipments:** delivered trips with the proof of delivery (photo, signature or signed waybill).
- **Balance owed:** what is still to pay across all invoices.

You only ever see your own company's shipments and documents.

## Emails you will get

- **Shipment booked:** your tracking code and a link to follow the trip.
- **Invoice:** the PDF attached, plus a download link that works for 30 days.
- **Tracking updates:** if you ask for them on the tracking page; the delivery email includes the proof of delivery.

## Help

Reply to any GEA email, or contact support@geahaulageandlogistics.com. To stop using the portal, ask GEA to switch off your access. You can ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted at any time (see the privacy policy).
