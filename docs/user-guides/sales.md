# Sales guide

You handle quote requests, follow-ups and website chats.

## New leads

Leads arrive from the website quote form, the website chat, and staff quick-add. Each new lead is shared out to Sales in turn, gets a follow-up due the next working day, and an email goes to the sales mailbox.

- **CRM → Leads** lists them. **Quick add lead** records a phone call, walk-in, WhatsApp or referral in three fields.
- Open a lead to see the details, log calls and messages under **Activities**, and set **Follow-ups**.
- **Call within one working day.** Management is alerted about leads nobody has contacted after that.
- Chat leads show a **transcript** and an **intent score**. 70 or more means the customer gave a route, cargo, tonnage and a date soon: call them first.

## Pipeline

**CRM → Pipeline** shows leads by stage: New, Contacted, Qualified, Quoted, Won, Lost. Drag a card to move it, or use its **Move to** menu. Lost needs a reason. Won turns the lead into a customer.

## Quotes

**Finance → Quotes and proformas → New.** Add lines and VAT is worked out. Download the PDF or Word file and send it to the customer, then click **Mark sent**: the lead moves to Quoted with the quote's value. When the customer agrees, click **Turn into invoice**.

## Website chats

The website assistant answers visitors from GEA's knowledge, tracks shipments, takes quote requests and books call-backs. It passes chats to a person for complaints, damage or loss, payments, disputes, or when asked.

1. You get a bell alert: **A website visitor needs a person**. **Assistant → Conversations** also shows a badge.
2. Open the chat, read the transcript, type your reply and click **Send reply**. The visitor sees "A GEA team member has joined the chat" and your message within a few seconds, while they are still on the website.
3. When finished, click **Hand back to assistant** or **Close chat**. If they left their phone number, call them.

Never type bank details, card numbers or prices into a chat; send prices as a quote.
