# Finance guide

You raise invoices, record payments and expenses, and run the finance reports. All amounts are exact to the kobo and VAT rounds half-up.

## Invoices

- **Delivered trips create a draft invoice** automatically (Haulage Trip template). Check the lines and click **Issue invoice**.
- To start from scratch: **Finance → Invoices → New**. Add lines (quantity × unit price), a discount if any, VAT rate and due date.
- **Issue invoice** gives the next number, `GEA-INV-2026-00001`, with no gaps. Drafts have no number.
- A discount over 10% needs Management to issue.
- **Email** sends the PDF from billing@, with a download link that works for 30 days.
- **Customer link (30 days)** gives a link to paste into WhatsApp. Anyone with the link can open that one invoice until it expires. **PDF** and **Word** download it. Select several invoices to download a ZIP.
- Five layouts: Corporate Classic, Bold Navy, Haulage Trip, E-Invoice (with QR code) and Proforma.
- Overdue invoices are marked every morning. Reminders go 3 days before the due date, on the due date, then every 7 days.

## Payments

**Finance → Payments → New.** Choose the customer, amount, date and method. The payment pays the oldest invoices first unless you choose which invoices. Record **WHT** deducted by the customer per invoice. Money left over stays as customer credit.

## Voiding and credit notes

You cannot delete an issued invoice. **Void** it with a reason (Management). If money was received, raise a **credit note** first.

## Expenses

**Finance → Expenses → New** with category, amount, date and receipt photo. Link the truck and trip where you can, so profit per truck and per trip is right. Truck maintenance logged by Operations is added automatically.

## Reports

**Finance → Finance reports:** revenue by month, customer and route; expenses by category; profit per truck and per trip; receivables ageing (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days); VAT and WHT summaries. Download as CSV, PDF or Word for your accountant.

You get a bell alert when an invoice becomes overdue.
