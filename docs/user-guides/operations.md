# Operations guide

You book shipments, assign trucks and drivers, keep tracking up to date and look after the fleet.

## Book a shipment

1. **Operations → Shipments → New shipment.** Choose the customer, describe the cargo, enter tonnage, origin, destination and pickup date. Towns are found on the map automatically.
2. Save. The shipment gets a tracking code like `GEA-2611-AB3CD`.
3. Open the shipment and click **Assign truck**. Only available trucks big enough for the load are listed. The truck's usual driver is filled in.
4. Use the **WhatsApp** button to send the customer their tracking link.

## Keep tracking up to date

Trips are updated three ways, and the customer's tracking page shows the newest:

- **Driver app:** drivers with smartphones send GPS every minute and tap the stage buttons themselves.
- **Update console** (Operations → Update console): for drivers without the app. Trips with no recent update are at the top. Call the driver, pick the town they are near and the stage, add a note, save. Two clicks.
- **Tracker companies:** if set up under Settings → Integrations, positions arrive automatically.

The **Dispatch board** shows every trip by stage. The **Live map** shows where active trucks are.

## Stages

Booked → Assigned → Loading → Loaded → In transit → Arrived → Delivered → Closed. A trip can be **Delayed** (give the reason; the customer sees it) and back to In transit. Only Operations or Management can cancel, with a reason.

**Delivered needs proof:** a photo or signature from the driver app, or upload a scan of the signed waybill.

## Alerts you will get

- A trip in transit has had no update for an hour.
- A trip is marked Delayed.
- A driver reports a **breakdown, accident, delay or checkpoint** from the app. Breakdowns and accidents are not shown to the customer until you decide what to tell them. See them on the shipment under **Driver reports** and click **Mark resolved** when done.

## Fleet

- **Fleet → Trucks:** documents (insurance, roadworthiness, licence) with expiry warnings 30 days ahead, and the maintenance log. Maintenance costs post to expenses automatically.
- **Fleet → Drivers:** licence expiry, usual truck, whether they have the app. **Set app PIN** gives a driver a new 4 to 6 digit PIN and signs out their old phone. Read the PIN to the driver; it is stored hidden.
- **Operations → Checkpoints and towns:** the list used by the update console. Add the towns your drivers pass.
